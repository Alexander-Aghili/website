    About this map

    This map brings together locations from Paul Freeman’s Abandoned & Little-Known Airfields. Each airfield name links to its original source in a new tab. The stories and photographs remain on Freeman’s website.

    Reading the map

    Round markers use coordinates supplied by the source. Diamond markers indicate explicit location uncertainty. Source coordinates are not independently verified. Unlocated entries remain searchable; no substitute coordinates are invented.

    Satellite imagery shows present-day landscapes from Esri World Imagery; imagery dates and resolution vary. It is not a historical overlay.
