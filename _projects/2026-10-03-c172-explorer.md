The Cessna 172 Systems Explorer takes apart the most-built airplane in history, one system per chapter: the airframe structure, flight controls, the gravity-fed fuel system and carburetor, the electrical system with every wire in the service manual, radios and antennas, the Lycoming O-320 down to its bolts, and the instruments.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/C172/c172-structure.png" alt="The explorer's structure chapter, showing the wing spars, ribs, and fuselage frame with a step note beside them." class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

Click any part to see what it is and where its position comes from: a published figure, the model's geometry, or an estimate. You can isolate parts, search for them, and export the model as glTF, OBJ, or STL with a parts list. Chrome and Edge render it with WebGPU; other browsers fall back to WebGL.

[Open the explorer]({{ '/c172/' | relative_url }}) · [Source code](https://github.com/Alexander-Aghili/VisualizationDisplaySystem)

The airframe and cockpit models come from the FlightGear Cessna 172P by the c172p-team (GPL-2.0). Facts come from the Cessna 172 service manual and the Lycoming parts catalog, and diagrams from the FAA Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge.
